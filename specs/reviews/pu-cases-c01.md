## Changes

- PU-C09-Q2 (stem rewritten, key unchanged). The old stem named the terminal bronchioles and added "where the conducting airways end", which made the item one-step recall of a histology label. The stem now describes the airway by position (the last purely conducting airway, just proximal to the first airway with alveoli opening from its wall), so the student must first identify the terminal bronchiole and then recall its lining (simple cuboidal with ciliated and club cells). The first approach step was updated to match.
- PU-C04-Q1 (fact corrected). The whyNot for the PTHrP distractor said flatly that PTHrP "does not raise 1,25-dihydroxyvitamin D". In humoral hypercalcemia of malignancy calcitriol is usually low or normal, but the absolute claim is not safe. Changed to "It usually leaves 1,25-dihydroxyvitamin D low or normal".
- PU-C05-Q4 (fact corrected, DOK). The why tied expiratory wheezing only to loss of radial traction at low lung volume. Added the main reason: during forced expiration, rising pleural pressure compresses the airways. DOK changed from 3 to 2, since this is a direct application of Poiseuille law.
- PU-C08-Q2 (claim removed). The why said panacinar emphysema is worst in the lower lobes "which receive more blood flow and therefore more neutrophils". That mechanism is a hypothesis that is not stated in the standard references, so I removed it and kept "It is usually most severe in the lower lobes."
