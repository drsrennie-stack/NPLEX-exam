# Review log: notes/chapters/PU-6.json (Phase 1)

- C01 physical limits paragraph: corrected what sets RV. In young people the chest wall resisting further compression is the main limit, and airway closure with gas trapping dominates with age and in obstructive disease. The earlier wording made airway closure primary at all ages.
- C02 dead space paragraph: fixed the ambiguous "the two are nearly equal", which could be read as anatomic and alveolar dead space being equal. It now says alveolar dead space is normally negligible, so physiologic and anatomic dead space nearly match.
- C04 equal pressure point paragraph: replaced "limits flow at lower volumes" with "flow becomes limited over more of the expiration". When the equal pressure point moves toward the alveoli, flow limitation begins at higher lung volumes, not lower ones.
- C05 Hering-Breuer paragraph: removed "helps raise rate during exercise". Guyton and Hall describe the reflex as mainly protective against overinflation, and I could not confirm the exercise role against the standard references.
