## Changes
- MS-8 refs: the Lippincott chapter title was wrong. Changed "Fatty Acid, Ketone Body, and Triacylglycerol Metabolism" to "Fatty Acid, Triacylglycerol, and Ketone Body Metabolism".
- MS-9 notes (OI): "the bad chain enters most triple helices" is only true for COL1A1 (3 of 4 molecules; COL1A2 spoils 1 of 2). Now reads "many triple helices (3 of 4 when COL1A1 is hit)".
- MS-9 tricks: "My Tonia, My Testicles, My Toupee, My Cataracts" changed from "mnemonic" to "trick". The standard First Aid form ends with a different item, so this variant cannot be called the established version. The content is accurate.
- MS-9 highYield: rewrote "depends on the reading frame, not on deletion size" to remove the "X, not Y" construction. The meaning is unchanged.
- MS-10 chart (junction disorders), botulism "Repeated effort": the claim "Little change at the bedside" could not be confirmed. Changed it to "Not a useful bedside clue".
- MS-10 chart, botulism "Reflexes": "Reduced" changed to "Normal or reduced". Reflexes are often preserved early in botulism.
- MS-10 tricks: the reflex mnemonic ("S1, 2 buckle my shoe...") was expanded only to single roots, which did not match the rhyme. It now gives S1 to S2, L3 to L4, C5 to C6, and C7 to C8, then names the main roots (S1, L4, C5 to C6, C7).
- MS-13-A chart (lab patterns), CKD alkaline phosphatase: "High" changed to "Normal or high". It is normal in low-turnover (adynamic) bone disease.
- MS-13-B chart (weakness or girdle pain), ESR: dermatomyositis and polymyositis changed from "Often high" to "Normal or high", because ESR is often normal in myositis. Steroid myopathy changed from "Normal" to "Not raised by the myopathy itself", because the patient's underlying disease can raise ESR.
