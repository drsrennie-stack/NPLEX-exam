| EN-2 | notes "Parathyroid and islets", bullet 2 | "Appear after puberty" is imprecise; oxyphil cells begin to appear around puberty. | Now "appear around puberty and increase with age". |
| EN-4 | pitfalls line 3 | "Norepinephrine can be made without it, epinephrine cannot" was an overstatement of PNMT dependence. | Now states PNMT is induced by cortisol-rich cortical blood, and extra-adrenal chromaffin tissue lacking this makes mainly norepinephrine. |
