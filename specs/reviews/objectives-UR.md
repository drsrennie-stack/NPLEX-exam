# Review log: content/objectives/UR.json

Every objective was checked for accuracy against standard references (Moore, Langman, Junqueira, Guyton and Hall, Robbins, Abbas, Jawetz, First Aid-level consensus), for measurable wording, scope, tags, and blueprint coverage. The USMLE Step 1 outline (Renal & Urinary System and the fluid, electrolyte, and acid-base part of Multisystem) was rechecked; the crosswalk categories match it.

## Facts corrected

- UR-7.1: The old text said intercalated cells make ammonium from glutamine. Ammoniagenesis from glutamine (and the new bicarbonate it yields) happens in the proximal tubule; intercalated cells secrete H+. Rewritten so the kidney excretes H+ as titratable acid (mainly phosphate) and as ammonium made from glutamine in the proximal tubule.
- UR-12-A.8: The old text said efferent arteriolar hyalinosis causes hyperfiltration as a single causal chain. Robbins describes hyperfiltration, nonenzymatic glycation, and hyaline arteriolosclerosis of both afferent and efferent arterioles as contributors. Rewritten to name all three without the contested single-cause claim.
- UR-12-A.7: Outdated MPGN nomenclature. "Type I" removed; dense deposit disease now labeled as a C3 glomerulopathy (current Robbins classification), still linked to C3 nephritic factor.
