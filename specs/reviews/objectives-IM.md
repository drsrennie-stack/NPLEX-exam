# IM objectives review

All 129 objectives in content/objectives/IM.json checked against Robbins, Abbas, Guyton and Hall, Moore, Langman, Jawetz, and First Aid level consensus, plus the USMLE Step 1 Content Outline. Coverage of every IM-15 condition and every element of IM-1 to IM-14 confirmed. Crosswalk and excluded lists checked; no ids changed.

## Facts corrected

- IM-1.3: DiGeorge parathyroids are usually hypoplastic, not always absent. Changed "parathyroid aplasia" to "parathyroid hypoplasia or aplasia".
- IM-12.4: Hyper-IgM syndrome has normal or high IgM, not always high. Changed to "normal or high IgM".
- IM-15-A.1: "Onset after childhood" was too absolute; CVID also presents in children. Changed to "its frequent onset in adulthood".
- IM-15-B.4: Said viral load is used "to stage disease". HIV is staged by CD4 count; viral load measures replication. Rewrote to "CD4 count for staging, viral load for replication" and added that the antigen and antibody screen is followed by the HIV-1 and HIV-2 differentiation assay.
- IM-15-E.2: Said AL amyloidosis is "often with multiple myeloma". Most AL patients have a plasma cell dyscrasia without overt myeloma. Changed to "a plasma cell dyscrasia, sometimes with multiple myeloma".
- IM-15-C.15: Listed nickel contact dermatitis under drug hypersensitivity; nickel is not a drug. Replaced with contact dermatitis from topical neomycin (type IV).

## Objectives rewritten

- IM-4.3: "Anal canal above versus below the pectinate line" named no nodes, so a question writer could not tell the expected answer. Added the destinations (internal iliac above, superficial inguinal below).
- IM-12.6: Made the cell cycle and proliferation element of the IM-12 statement explicit by stating that Fas-mediated apoptosis ends lymphocyte clonal expansion.
- IM-14.4: IM-14 had no objective for the rubella virus, a pathogen of a listed condition (IM-15-F8). Added rubella virus (enveloped positive-sense RNA, formerly classed as a togavirus, reflecting its current reclassification) to the measles and mumps objective, and tightened the paramyxovirus wording.
- IM-14.5: "The herpesviruses in this unit" was vague. Named them as the herpesviruses behind mononucleosis and roseola and added HHV-6 and HHV-7 tropism for CD4 T cells.
- IM-15-C.4: Added the current name "anti-GBM disease" alongside Goodpasture syndrome.
- IM-15-E.4: Replaced the outdated "senile cardiac" with "wild-type age-related cardiac" transthyretin amyloidosis.
