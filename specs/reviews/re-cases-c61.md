- RE-C67-Q4 (fact corrected, stem rewritten): the stem said villous trophoblast lacks HLA-A and HLA-B and is protected from uterine NK cells by HLA-G. - RE-C69-Q3 (fact corrected, stem rewritten): the stem asked which blastocyst part gave rise to "the molar villous tissue".
