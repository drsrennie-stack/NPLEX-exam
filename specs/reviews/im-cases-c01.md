## Changes

- IM-C10-Q1 (stem, approach, whyNot): C1q deficiency was arguably defensible for recurrent pneumococcal infection plus immune complex glomerulonephritis (C1q deficiency also gives glomerulonephritis and some bacterial infections). Added "Total hemolytic complement (CH50) and alternative pathway hemolytic activity (AH50) are both undetectable," which rules out C1q and C1 esterase inhibitor and leaves C3 as the single best answer over C7 on the infection pattern. Approach and the whyNot entries for C1q, C7, and C1 esterase inhibitor rewritten to use the new data.
- IM-C10 (case): patient was 17 years old, but the SIRS criteria applied in IM-C10-Q3 (pulse above 90/min, respirations above 20/min, leukocytes above 12,000/mm3) are adult criteria; pediatric SIRS thresholds differ for ages 13 to 17. Changed to "A 20-year-old man" so the adult criteria and adult reference ranges apply correctly.
- IM-C04-Q1 (stem): "Flow cytometry ... Which of the following findings is most likely?" did not match the options, which describe mechanisms (failure to become plasma cells cannot be seen on flow cytometry). Changed to "Which of the following best describes the defect underlying her immunodeficiency?"
- IM-C04-Q3 (stem): "Clearance of Giardia ... depends largely on secretory IgA" overstated the evidence (T cell and IL-17 responses also matter). Changed to "Secretory IgA helps clear Giardia from the small intestine."
- IM-C07-Q1 (whyNot for 75%): said 75% "would require both parents to pass on a mutant allele," which is not a correct derivation. Rewritten: 75% is the chance a child of two carriers gets at least one mutant allele in autosomal recessive inheritance, which does not fit an X-linked disease with an unaffected father.
- IM-C07-Q2 (stem): "His low IgM reflects poor antibody responses to polysaccharide antigens" stated an unconfirmed causal link. Changed to "He also has poor antibody responses to polysaccharide antigens."
- IM-C07-Q4 (stem): "His small platelets are removed mainly in the spleen" overstated it; Wiskott-Aldrich thrombocytopenia also reflects ineffective platelet production. Changed to "Many of his small platelets are destroyed in the spleen."
- IM-C08-Q1 (whyNot for G6PD): ended with "the X-linked NADPH oxidase defect is the cause of CGD," which was confusing because G6PD deficiency is also X-linked. Rewritten: CGD is a defect of NADPH oxidase itself.
- IM-C08-Q2 (whyNot for lipooligosaccharide): said lipooligosaccharide "belongs to Neisseria," implying it is unique to them; Haemophilus also has it. Corrected to "the outer membrane endotoxin of Neisseria and Haemophilus."
- IM-C08-Q3 (stem): the opening sentence listed "phagolysosome fusion" (the key) as intact in the index patient, which cued the answer and was unnecessary. Removed that sentence; the Chediak-Higashi vignette stands on its own.
