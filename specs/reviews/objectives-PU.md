## Facts corrected

- PU-1.4: Said surfactant production begins "around week 26". First Aid puts the start near week 20 and Langman near the end of the sixth month, so a single week number is not standard. Now reads "beginning in the second trimester and reaching mature levels by about week 35".
- PU-10.4: Implied benzo[a]pyrene adducts mutate both p53 and Rb. The standard teaching is the TP53 mutation signature from tobacco adducts; RB1 loss is a small cell carcinoma feature covered in PU-10.5. Now reads "cause mutations, including in TP53".
- PU-13.5: Called respiratory syncytial virus a paramyxovirus. RSV has been reclassified into the family Pneumoviridae. Now reads "a pneumovirus, formerly classed as a paramyxovirus".
- PU-14-H.2: Used "Chlamydophila pneumoniae". The genus has been folded back into Chlamydia, so it now reads "Chlamydia pneumoniae". The USMLE heading text was left as written in the outline.
- PU-14-A.1: Listed bronchopulmonary dysplasia and retinopathy of prematurity as complications of neonatal respiratory distress syndrome itself. Both are complications of the oxygen therapy used to treat it. Now predicts patent ductus arteriosus, plus bronchopulmonary dysplasia and retinopathy of prematurity "from oxygen therapy".

## Objectives rewritten (specificity, coverage, or style)

- PU-13.6: Promised to distinguish the dimorphic fungi "by geography" but gave no geography for Blastomyces. Added "Great Lakes and Ohio and Mississippi basins".
- PU-14-A.5: Named Lofgren syndrome with nothing testable. Added its features (erythema nodosum, hilar lymphadenopathy, arthritis, fever) and moved erythema nodosum out of the general sarcoidosis list.
- PU-14-A.3: "Patchy subpleural fibroblastic foci" blurred the usual interstitial pneumonia pattern. Now reads "patchy subpleural and basal fibrosis, fibroblastic foci, honeycombing".
- PU-8.6: Changed the soft verb "recognize mixed disturbances" to "identify mixed disturbances". PU-14-H.2 also gained "which lacks a cell wall" for Mycoplasma pneumoniae, so the morphology of an atypical pneumonia organism is testable (PU-13 had no Mycoplasma objective).
