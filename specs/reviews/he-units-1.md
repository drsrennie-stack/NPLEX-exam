## Changes

| Unit | Field | What was wrong | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| HE-1 | notes "Lineages from the stem cell", bullets 2 and 3 | "Monocytes later become tissue macrophages" sat in the common lymphoid progenitor bullet, which implied monocytes are lymphoid. | Moved to the common myeloid bullet ("monocytes (which become tissue macrophages)"); lymphoid bullet now lists only B, T, and NK cells. |
| HE-2 | notes "Reading the CBC", bullet 1 | Objective HE-2.5 names hematocrit, but no hematocrit value was taught. | Added hematocrit reference ranges (about 41 to 53% in men, 36 to 46% in women). |
| HE-2 | notes "Albumin", bullet 2 | "Calcium (about 40% of total calcium)" credited albumin with all protein-bound calcium. | Now "most of the protein-bound calcium, which is about 40% of the total". |
| HE-2 | highYield line 2 | "Every form of secondary polycythemia has high erythropoietin" is an overstatement (for example androgen-driven or high-affinity hemoglobin cases can show normal levels). | Now limited to secondary polycythemia from hypoxia or an erythropoietin-secreting tumor. |
| HE-3 | chart "Intravascular versus extravascular hemolysis", haptoglobin row | Extravascular haptoglobin given as "Normal or mildly low"; haptoglobin is often clearly low in extravascular hemolysis too, only less markedly. | Now "Normal or low (less marked)". |
| HE-4 | notes "Secondary hemostasis", bullet 1 | Tissue factor with VIIa was said to activate only factor X; it also activates factor IX. | Now "activates factors X and IX". |
| HE-4 | chart "Localizing a bleeding disorder", von Willebrand row | Key clue given as "Low factor VIII activity", which is variable and not the defining lab. | Now "Low von Willebrand factor activity (ristocetin cofactor); factor VIII may be low". |
| HE-6 | pitfalls line 4 | Wording ("Hemophilia daughters of an affected father...") was garbled and hard to read. | Rewritten plainly: all daughters of a father with hemophilia are carriers, and no sons are affected because sons get his Y chromosome. |
| HE-7 | notes "Autoimmune hemolytic anemia", bullet 4 | Said cold agglutinins bind "best below about 30 °C"; they bind best near 4 °C and act at temperatures up to about 30 °C. | Now "binds red cells at temperatures below about 30 °C (86 °F)". |
| HE-7 | notes "Antiphospholipid syndrome", bullet 2 | Said lupus anticoagulant "binds the phospholipid" in the reagent; the antibodies target phospholipid-binding proteins and interfere with phospholipid-dependent steps. | Now "interferes with the phospholipid-dependent steps of the PTT". |
