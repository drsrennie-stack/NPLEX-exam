# CV objectives review

Reviewed all 193 original objectives against specs/OBJECTIVES.md, the CV blueprint units in nplex-competencies.js, and the USMLE Step 1 Content Outline (Cardiovascular section plus the Multisystem, Blood, Immune, Respiratory, and Skin items that name CV conditions). No ids were renumbered; the two new objectives were appended at the end of their units.

## Facts corrected

- CV-1.1: Said the heart tube fuses in week 4. The endocardial tubes fuse by the end of week 3 and looping starts in week 4 (Langman). "Left-right dynein" was not a real term. Rewrote with fusion by end of week 3, rightward looping in week 4, and defective ciliary dynein (Kartagener syndrome).
- CV-1.7: Said the sixth arch forms "the pulmonary arteries". It forms only the proximal pulmonary arteries (and the ductus). Changed to "proximal pulmonary arteries".
- CV-7.7: Defined sick sinus syndrome only as alternating bradycardia and tachycardia, which is one form. Changed to SA node dysfunction causing sinus bradycardia, sinus pauses, or alternating bradycardia and tachycardia.
- CV-10.5: Said lymphedema is firm nonpitting swelling; early lymphedema pits. Changed to swelling that becomes firm and nonpitting as fibrosis develops.
- CV-15.6: Listed clot filtering as a metabolic role of the pulmonary endothelium. Filtering is done by the capillary bed and is not metabolic. Changed to "nonrespiratory roles of the pulmonary circulation".
- CV-18-D.6: Said Libman-Sacks vegetations sit on both leaflet surfaces. Robbins: either or both surfaces. Changed to "small sterile vegetations on either or both leaflet surfaces".
- CV-18-K.5: Listed DIC as a complication of Rocky Mountain spotted fever. Overt DIC is rare in RMSF despite thrombocytopenia. Replaced with multiorgan failure.

## Objectives rewritten

- CV-7.6: USMLE outline names multifocal atrial tachycardia and paroxysmal tachycardias, which no objective covered. Added both to the ECG rhythm list.
- CV-16.4: Called congenital heart block a "pericardial and endocardial" disease. Changed to "pericardial, valvular, and conduction disease".
- CV-17.1: The CV-17 statement requires morphology, and the endocarditis organisms had no morphology. Added gram-positive cocci in clusters (S. aureus) and cocci in chains (viridans streptococci) and framed as morphology and virulence factors.
- CV-17.6: The CV-17 statement requires genetic characteristics and transmission; coxsackie B had neither. Added nonenveloped positive-sense RNA picornavirus and fecal-oral spread.
- CV-18-E.3: Ended with an ungrammatical clause ("and right-sided congestion"). Rewrote to state diastolic filling failure with right-sided congestion, then the low-voltage ECG with a thick wall in amyloidosis.

## Objectives added

- CV-16.7 (M, dok 3, Both, Immune System: HIV/AIDS: Kaposi sarcoma): The CV-16 statement names immune activation by cancer and tumors, and no CV-16 objective covered it. Added lost T-cell surveillance letting HHV-8 Kaposi sarcoma grow, regression when immunity returns, and atrial myxoma IL-6 causing constitutional symptoms.
- CV-18-E.6 (X, dok 2, USMLE, cond []): USMLE outline names takotsubo cardiomyopathy and tachycardia-induced heart failure, which no objective covered. Added as a USMLE extension in primary myocardial diseases.
