## Changes

- NE-C41-Q4 (whyNot, esophagus): "upper thoracic segments" was vague and partly wrong (esophageal afferents enter at mid-thoracic levels). Changed to "through segments of about T4 to T6, well above T10".
- NE-C42-Q1 (tag): sea was P, but spinal shock and the phases of cord transection are pathophysiology (objective NE-20-H.4 is sea X). Changed sea to X. The case still mixes three SEAs (X, P, A).
- NE-C42-Q3 (why): the text said bladder pain never reaches awareness because visceral afferents run with sympathetic nerves. Bladder pain afferents largely travel with pelvic splanchnic (parasympathetic) nerves, and the real reason is that they enter the cord below the T4 lesion. Rewrote the sentence to say that.
- NE-C42-Q4 (why): the text said the corticospinal tract and dorsal columns "have not crossed yet at the spinal level". The corticospinal tract has already crossed (pyramidal decussation) before it reaches the cord. Rewrote: at the spinal level both tracts serve the same side of the body, and they cross in the medulla.
- NE-C44 (case summary): "(startle myoclonus)" was a parenthetical label in the exam-voice case, against the no-hints-in-parentheses rule. Changed to "Sudden noises cause brief myoclonic jerks of her arms."
- NE-C44-Q1 (why): the text said variant CJD comes from beef "or from contaminated neurosurgical instruments". Instrument transmission is iatrogenic CJD, not variant CJD. Corrected to name variant CJD (bovine prions in beef) and iatrogenic CJD (contaminated neurosurgical instruments or dura mater grafts) separately.
- NE-C44-Q2 (rewritten): the item asked which rhythm dominates in a relaxed adult with eyes closed, which is single-step recall (not allowed). Rewrote it so the student must identify the starting rhythm (alpha, eyes closed and relaxed) and predict the change when the eyes open and she does mental arithmetic (alpha replaced by low-voltage beta, alpha blocking). New options, approach, why, and whyNot written; obj NE-11.5 and DOK 2 kept.
- NE-C46-Q3 (stem): the patient's sex was implied only by "she", and "when she works overhead" did not fit a swimmer. Changed to "A 24-year-old woman who is a competitive swimmer", put "right" on the forearm and hand findings, and changed to "when she raises it overhead".
- NE-C47 (case summary): "A 44-year-old warehouse worker" gave no sex in the opening. Changed to "A 44-year-old man who works in a warehouse".
- NE-C48-Q4 (stem): vision loss "began 1 hour ago". In the first hour of central retinal artery occlusion the fundus can still look near normal, which made the "normal retina with a bright plaque in an arteriole" distractor defensible. Changed the onset to 5 hours ago, when retinal whitening and the cherry-red spot are expected.
