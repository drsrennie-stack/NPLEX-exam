- GI-3.2: "Lower rectum" was listed as retroperitoneal, but the lower third of the rectum lies below the peritoneum. Changed to "part of the rectum".
- GI-13-C.8: Used outdated nomenclature. Changed to metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD, formerly nonalcoholic fatty liver disease) and steatohepatitis (MASH).
