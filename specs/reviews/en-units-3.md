## Changes

- EN-12-D, notes "Acute crisis: DKA and HHS": said high glucagon drives lipolysis. Lipolysis is unleashed by insulin lack; glucagon drives hepatic ketogenesis. Reworded to say that.
- EN-12-D, notes "Why chronic hyperglycemia damages tissue": the "Results" bullet read as if the polyol pathway caused accelerated atherosclerosis. Now assigns cataracts and neuropathy to the polyol pathway and atherosclerosis (MI, stroke, PAD) to AGEs and vessel injury.
- EN-12-D, highYield: called Kimmelstiel-Wilson nodules pathognomonic. Similar nodules occur in light chain deposition disease and amyloidosis. Changed to "highly characteristic" and named nodular glomerulosclerosis.
- EN-12-D, chart "Type 1 vs type 2 diabetes", Acute crisis row: type 2 listed only HHS. Added that DKA can still occur in type 2 under severe stress.
- EN-12-E, chart "Thyroglossal duct cyst vs branchial cleft cyst", Lining row: branchial cleft cyst lining given as squamous only. Robbins describes squamous or pseudostratified columnar lining. Now "Squamous or respiratory epithelium, lymphoid tissue in wall".
- EN-12-E, trick "Follow the thyroid's trip": said a pyramidal lobe can appear anywhere on the descent path "and nowhere else". The pyramidal lobe arises from the isthmus, and rare ectopic thyroid occurs off this path. Rewritten to say a cyst or ectopic thyroid tissue (such as a lingual thyroid) lies on the midline path.
- EN-12-E, pitfalls: called branchial cleft cysts "fixed". They are mobile; they just do not rise with swallowing or tongue protrusion. Reworded.
- EN-12-F, trick "Portal means vulnerable": said the posterior lobe "keeps making ADH". ADH is made in the hypothalamus and released from the posterior lobe. Changed to "releasing".
- EN-12-G, notes "Parathyroid and pituitary tumors" (coverage, objective EN-12-G.9): craniopharyngioma bullet lacked headache and bitemporal hemianopia. Added both.
- EN-12-G, chart "Pituitary and adrenal tumor quick view", Craniopharyngioma row: added headache and bitemporal hemianopia to match.
