How it works

Each card shows you a prompt with a directive about what to retrieve. Predict the answer out loud or on paper, then reveal and rate how cleanly you recalled it on a 4-button scale. The SM-2 algorithm uses your ratings to schedule the next encounter: clean retrievals move further apart in time, missed ones come back sooner. The aim is maximum retention with minimum time spent on what you already own.

Pair this tool with the master study guide. Use the guide for first-encounter reading and reference; use Spaced Recall for active retrieval practice. Your progress saves automatically in your browser. Nothing is sent to any server.

Daily limits

New cards per day
How many never-seen cards to introduce in each session. Increase if you have weeks until your exam; decrease if you are getting overwhelmed.

A ceiling on the number of due cards per session. Prevents review backlogs from creating unmanageable sessions.
