How it works

Session flow
  1. 1
    Configure your session. Pick a Part, a session mode, categories or modalities, count, depth, and grading behavior. The engine builds a single AI-runnable prompt.
  2. 2
    Paste into Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or Copilot. The AI runs your session live.
  3. 3
    Tutoring mode. One question at a time. Answer with reasoning. The AI grades immediately, gives a rationale, and (if enabled) offers one retry with a minimal hint on incorrect answers.
  4. 4
    Mini-exam mode. Blueprint-proportional within the categories you select. No feedback during. Submit all answers at the end. The AI grades everything at once and runs an automatic diagnostic. Use this when you want a practice exam rather than a study session.
  5. 5
    End-of-session scoring sheet. NPLEX-aligned: GEA pass/fail with raw and converted scores, SEA breakdown, weak topics, and a priority study action. In mini-exam mode the diagnostic analysis is automatic. Copy or print.
Recommended runtime: Claude. Claude follows the long structured prompt most faithfully across a full session, holds the balanced answer key without drifting to B or C, and writes homeopathy and botanical questions from the classical materia medica without injecting skeptical framing into the rationale. ChatGPT is a strong alternative, especially for lab and pharmacology items where precise numeric ranges matter. Gemini and Copilot work but produce noticeably weaker NPLEX-specific results.
